Procedure Order for Axle Counter Reset

SOFT RESET OPERATION PROTOCOLS

Return to Current Version (Rev-01)
INTRODUCTION & APPLICABILITY: This document contains the Procedure Order for axle counter soft reset from ATS MMI/VDU provided in SCR/OCC/BCC. This Procedure Order should be read in conjunction with MRGR 2013 and other relevant instructions. This document is applicable to Traffic Controller, Station Controller & other O&M staff.

I. Requirements & Effects of Soft Reset

Requirement for Reset

If track section is flashing due to power failure / cold start of axle counter section / miscount or any other error, soft reset operation is required.

System Behavior After Reset

  • System will reset count on the evaluator but the state of the section will remain occupied until a valid check-in/check-out is completed.
  • Valid check-in/check-out requires a train sweep through the fault section (can be done in RM with any train type).
  • In a point section, sweeping in only one direction of point is sufficient.

Effect of Failure

  • Axle counters (AX) are secondary train detection. Failure does not cause failure in primary system (CBTC radio). Both are independent.
  • Green aspect of concerned route will not be available; Non-CBTC train proceeds as per procedure.

II. Roles & Responsibility: Initial Verification

Axle Counter Soft Reset command will be given by Traffic Controller/Station Controller (having the control of interlocking). TC & SC shall take the following steps:

  • STEP 1: TRAIN COUNT & VALIDATION. TC shall count the number of operating trains between adjacent stations of the failed section by monitoring ATS MMI/Video Wall and verify train IDs. SC/s of concerned interlocking station/s shall take same action simultaneously for validation by comparing indications on LATS/VDU.
  • STEP 2: STATION HOLD. TC/SC shall place station hold for train running in ATO/ATP in station preceding failure. Train running in degraded mode shall be stopped as per extant procedure.
  • STEP 3: LOCATION CONFIRMATION. TC/SC shall obtain current locations of all trains present in the affected section... on train radio and ensure that no train is present in the section between adjacent stations.

III. Roles & Responsibility: Command & Sweeping

Step 4 & 5: Issuing the Reset Command

  • If track section failure is ascertained positively and no train is present... TC/SC shall give Axle Counter Soft reset command.
  • HANDLING AMBIGUITY: In case of any ambiguity regarding vacancy... TC shall ask Station Controller to verify it physically. After physical verification that no train is present... SC/TC will give Axle Counter soft reset command.

Step 6: Train Sweep Procedure

After giving soft reset command, a train running through (sweep in RM) the fault section is required in order to clear occupancy of the section.

  • TC shall instruct TO of the train required for sweeping the section, to change the mode in RM.
  • TC shall ensure from ATS terminal that the mode of the train is changed to RM and then give permission to TO for running the train in RM till next station.

IV. Important Limitations & Failure Management

Detection Limitations

Any vehicle with wheel diameter less than 300 mm will not be detected by Axle Counter.

Hard Fault Management

In case soft reset of Axle counter section is not possible due to any technical reason, TC/SC shall inform signalling maintenance staff to attend the fault/failure.
